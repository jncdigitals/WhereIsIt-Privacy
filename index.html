WhereIsIt Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

WhereIsIt is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information stored by the app

The app may store item names, locations, notes, and photos that you choose to add. This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Accounts

WhereIsIt does not require an account or registration.

Analytics

WhereIsIt does not use analytics or tracking services.

Advertising

WhereIsIt does not display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

Third-party services

The app does not send your stored information to third-party services.

Data deletion

You can delete saved items directly from the app. Uninstalling WhereIsIt removes the app's locally stored data from your device.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
